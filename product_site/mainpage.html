What is MultiWatch?

MultiWatch is a simple way to keep track of every show you are currently watching, no matter which streaming service it's on. Instead of trying to remember what you left off on Netflix, Hulu, and HBO Max separately, MultiWatch brings it all together into one single, easy-to-use list. Whether you're juggling three shows or ten, MultiWatch keeps everything organized in one place so you always know what you're watching, where you're watching it, and how far along you are. No more switching between apps or scrolling through your memory trying to remember which episode you left off on.

Stop losing track of your shows! With MultiWatch, you can finally stay organized and never forget where you left off. Our goal is to make managing your watchlist across multiple platforms fast, simple, and stress-free. As more and more streaming services launch their own exclusive shows, keeping up with everything you watch has only gotten harder. MultiWatch solves that problem by giving you one central hub built specifically for people who watch shows across multiple platforms, so you can spend less time searching and more time actually watching.
