My Dish: Mangú

My favorite dish is a spanish dish called Mangú

This is my favorite dish because growing up my mom would make it and would be so good. Its a traditional Dominican dish made from boiled green plantains mashed into a smooth creamy puree, fried cheese, and friend salami with onion toppings.
